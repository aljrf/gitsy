## Why I Wake Early

_Page 12 • Mar 9, 2025_

Hello, sun in my face.Hello, you who make the morningand spread it over the fieldsand into the faces of the tulipsand the nodding morning glories,and into the windows of, even, themiserable and the crotchety—<br><br>best preacher that ever was,dear star, that just happensto be where you are in the universeto keep us from ever-darkness,to ease us with warm touching,to hold us in the great hands of light—good morning, good morning, good morning.<br><br>Watch, now, how I start the dayin happiness, in kindness.

## Some Things, Say the Wise Ones

_Page 78 • Mar 23, 2025_

Some Things, Say the Wise Ones<br><br>Some things, say the wise ones who know everything,are not living. I say,you live your life your way and leave me alone.<br><br>I have talked with the faint clouds in the sky when theyare afraid of being left behind; I have said, Hurry, hurry!and they have said: Thank you, we are hurrying.<br><br>About cows, and starfish, and roses, there is noargument. They die, after all.<br><br>But water is a question, so many living things in it,but what is it, itself, living or not? Oh, gleaming<br><br>generosity, how can they write you out?<br><br>As I think this I am sitting on the sand besidethe harbor. I am holding in my handsmall pieces of granite, pyrite, schist.Each one, just now, so thoroughly asleep.
