statue, for example, has (1) a material cause (the stone it is made out of), (2) an efficient cause (the sculptor who chisels it), (3) a formal cause (the precise design and shape of the statue which the sculptor implements), and (4) a final cause, which is the reason and purpose of its existence (to stand in a sanctuary and receive the offerings due to it). Potentiality, in a human, is closely allied to the final cause, which explains the reason and purpose of your existence. A human being’s material cause (1) is the organic matter – blood, flesh, bone – out which you are made. Your efficient cause (2) is the parents who made you. Your formal cause (3) is the DNA which determined your genetic make-up, appearance, and constitution. The only cause in your own control is your final cause (4), the reason and purpose of your existence
